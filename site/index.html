Your memories,
one wave at a time.

Swell turns your camera roll into a feed you scroll through. Now and then a wave washes over you: a day, a trip or a summer, gathered and told from beginning to end.

How it works

  • A feed of your own photosOld and new moments are mixed, so you find things you had forgotten. Swell learns what you like the more you watch.
  • WavesPhotos that belong together become a wave. The red thread shows how the day or the trip went, and where you are in it.
  • Private from the startYour photos stay on your phone. No ads, no tracking and no followers.
  • Share with the people you chooseSend a moment or a whole wave to a friend or a group. What you send disappears after 30 days.
