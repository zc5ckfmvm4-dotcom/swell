Privacy policy

Your photos are yours.

Last updated October 18, 2026

Swell is built to be private. Most of what the app does happens on your phone, and we only collect what's needed for you to share with your friends.

What stays on your phone

Swell reads your camera roll to show the feed and put together waves. The photos, videos and location data in them never leave your phone unless you send something yourself. The same goes for what you've liked, saved or hidden, and how the app learns what you enjoy. All of that is stored in the app on your phone.

If Back up to iCloud is on (it is by default, and you can turn it off in Swell's settings), a list of your albums, saved moments, saved waves, likes and hidden moments is also stored in your own iCloud account, so it can be restored on a new phone. It only contains references to which photos they are, never the photos themselves. It's stored by Apple in your iCloud, not on our server, and we can't see it.

If you turn on Likes become favorites in Photos, Swell marks those photos as favorites in the Photos app. If you delete a photo in Swell, it's removed from your photo library. iOS always asks first.

If you use friends and chat

Friends and chat are optional. If you use them, the following is stored on our server:

  • Account: an anonymous ID from Sign in with Apple, or your email address and password if you sign in with email. We never receive your Apple password.
  • Profile: the name and username you choose. Your friends can see them, and others can find you by your username.
  • Friends and groups: who you're friends with, your friend requests and invite codes, and which groups you're in.
  • Messages: what you write and send, and when it was read. If you send a photo, video or wave, its date and any place name come along, so the recipient sees when and where it was taken.
  • Reactions: the reaction you've added to a message. Everyone in the chat can see it.
  • Blocks and reports: who you've blocked, and reports you send (with a copy of the reported content, so we can review it).
  • Notifications: an address from Apple that lets your phone receive notifications about new messages.

Only the people in the same chat can see the messages. Photos, videos and waves you send are automatically deleted from the server 30 days after they were sent. The recipient can choose to save them to their Photos before then.

Who helps us

We never sell data and don't share it with advertisers. Two services help us run the app:

  • Supabase stores accounts, messages and sent files.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple and delivers notifications.

Swell has no ads and no tracking, and uses no analytics tools from other companies.

Delete your account

You can delete your account right in the app: Friends → My friends → the menu in the top right → Delete account. Your profile, friends, group memberships and notification addresses are removed immediately. Messages you've already sent remain with the recipients, but without your name. Photos, videos and waves still disappear no later than 30 days after they were sent.

The photos on your phone are not affected. If you delete the app, everything Swell stored on the phone is removed too.

Children

Swell is not intended for children under 13.
