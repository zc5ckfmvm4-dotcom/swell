Frequently asked questions

Swell doesn't show any photos

Swell needs access to your photos. Open Settings → Apps → Swell → Photos and choose Full Access. With limited access, only the photos you picked are shown.

When do the waves come?

Waves appear on their own as you scroll, a few moments into the feed. In the Waves tab you'll find the ones you've saved.

What does the red thread mean?

The thread has the shape of the wave: the peaks are when you took the most photos. The red part shows how far into the wave you are.

How long does what I send stay?

Photos, videos and waves are deleted from the server after 30 days. The recipient can save them to their Photos before then.

How do I block or report someone?

Hold a friend in your friends list and choose Block, or tap the menu in the top right of a chat. Hold a message to report it. We review reports within 24 hours. Read more in the terms.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Friends → My friends, tap the menu in the top right and choose Delete account. Read more in the privacy policy.

The tips only showed once

All tips are collected in the app's settings.
