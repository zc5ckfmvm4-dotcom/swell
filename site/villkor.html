Terms of use

Be kind.

Last updated October 16, 2026

Swell is for sharing memories with people you know. When you sign in to use friends and chat, you accept these terms.

What's not okay

We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and for users who behave badly. You may not send:

  • harassment, threats, bullying or hate against anyone,
  • sexual content without consent, or anything at all that sexualizes children,
  • violence, illegal content or anything that encourages crime,
  • spam, scams or other people's photos without permission.

Block and report

You can block someone at any time: hold a friend in the list, or tap the menu in a chat. Someone you've blocked can no longer send you anything.

Hold a message to report it, or report a person from the chat's menu. We review reports within 24 hours. We remove content that breaks these terms and remove the person who sent it.

Your content

What you send is yours. You only give us the right to store it and deliver it to the people you send it to. Photos, videos and waves are deleted from the server after 30 days. Read more in the privacy policy.

Age

You must be at least 13 years old to use friends and chat.

Suspension and deletion

We may suspend accounts that break these terms. You can delete your own account in the app whenever you like.
